### The two defects that mattered

**P0-7f** — the alignment endpoint resolved twin blocks by calling a dict as a function.
Falsy on all/industry/fte_band cuts, so five of eight signal mechanisms never fired;
`TypeError` on twin/group cuts, swallowed, so every area rendered blank. Both silent. The
document had been asserting less than it knew for as long as the resolver had been wrong.

**P1-B** — that failure was absorbed into an empty list and the paper still printed:
forty pages telling a board that eight areas flag nothing, behind one warning line. It now
fails the request and logs the org and the cut.

### The three defects the review process found in itself

1. **G65 was satisfied by its own comment.** The check owning P1-B asked `"raise" in
   <except body>`; the body's comment said "…what **raised** here before P0-7f". A mutant
   that deleted the raise passed. Red-first had not caught it because the check is a
   conjunction and only one clause had been tested — **red-first is per-clause**.
2. **The suite ran 34 of Gate A's 66 checks** and printed `ALL GATES GREEN`. Every
   artefact check — the whole v3.3 red-first block — was skipped, and nothing in the chain
   could tell "everything passed" from "less ran than you think".
3. **My own format audit reported a defect the artefact does not have.** F-011's title
   finding came from a harness render whose `<title>` is `h`. Arm C — fixture divergence —

## The close-out read changed the shape of this close-out

Five readers over the delivered pages — a non-executive director, the reward director,
the CFO, a Big-4 production lead, an accessibility reviewer — plus a critic looking for
what they all missed. **102 findings: 21 blockers, 59 serious, 22 polish.** Full list:
`CLOSE_OUT_READ_2026-08-17.md`.

**The finding that matters most: almost none of them are format.** This engagement spent
its last four classes on typography, glyphs, empty states, visuals and charts, and the
document that came out the other side still asks a committee to re-approve amendments it
never describes, describes the action it wants approved in three words, and asserts two
opposite things about family provision on facing pages. **The craft work was real and the
substance underneath it was not audited.**

Three claims were verified against the artefact before being recorded:

| claim | verdict |
|---|---|
| The position chart plots an aim marker on Wellbeing and Governance & transparency, which the document says have no stated aim | **CONFIRMED — and fixed.** The document states aims are set "for 6 of the 8"; neither area carries a "You aim … on <area>" sentence anywhere. The chart read `d.target.stance` off the hero domains, where the exclusion (`STRATEGY_POSITION_EXCLUDE`, app.py:5114) is not applied — so the two **widest apparent gaps on the one exhibit a committee looks at** were against aims nobody set. The marker now reads the commitments register, the same source as the sentences |
| p7 "Family provision is deliberately more generous than the market" vs p6 "Time off & family is aimed below market" and p12/p24 "You aim below market on Time off & family" | **CONFIRMED.** Direct contradiction, three pages apart, and the aim is stated twice more in the register |
| p7 asserts "Pay information is fully open" with the refutation buried on p13 | **PARTLY OVERTURNED.** p9 *does* carry it under "THE CONTRADICTIONS ON THE REGISTER". The substance stands — Part A asserts it flatly with no cross-reference — but the document is not silent about it |

The remaining 99 are the readers' assessments and have **not** been verified. They should
be triaged before any is treated as a defect: this engagement has already seen a review
claim overturned by measurement three times, and one of those was my own audit reporting a
defect the artefact does not have.

**What I would do next, and it is not more polish.** Take the 21 blockers, verify each
against the artefact the way the three above were verified, and separate them into: real
contradictions in the strategy content (the family-provision one is real), engine defects
(the aim marker was one), and reader misreadings. The format queue — F-016, F-001, the
rest — should wait behind that. A document that looks like a Big-4 paper and contradicts
itself on facing pages is worse than one that does neither, because the craft buys the
contradiction credibility.

## The habit worth keeping

Every substantive finding in this engagement came from **measuring the artefact rather
than reasoning about it** — and three of them were defects in the measuring apparatus,
found the same way. The verifier caught two page spills I would have shipped, a mutation
battery caught a check I had already committed, and reading computed style off the live
DOM named a CSS collision that looked exactly like a print-colour problem.

The corollary is the one to hold: **a green suite is a claim about what ran.** It was worth
34 of 66 for the whole engagement, and nothing said so.
