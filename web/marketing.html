Questions

The questions every HR team asks

I'm not a reward specialist — is this for me?

Yes. If a manager has asked whether you're competitive on benefits, leave or pension, lumi answers it in plain English — leads and gaps are called out for you, with no chart-decoding or analyst skills required and nothing to integrate. HRBPs use it to answer a question fast; Heads of Reward use it to prep the board.

Where does the data come from?

From members. lumi is a benchmarking co-operative: every organisation in the pool is a real UK employer that submits its own reward data. Nothing is scraped, purchased or modelled — which is why every figure can cite the number of organisations behind it.

How do you keep self-reported data reliable?

Every metric has one standard definition, so everyone answers the same question the same way; responses are validated as they're entered and out-of-range answers are flagged. Comparisons are medians and percentiles, so a single odd entry can't skew your read.

Can other members see our data?

No. Members only ever see anonymised aggregates — medians and distributions across the pool. Any figure resting on fewer than 5 organisations is suppressed, so no individual organisation's answers can be worked out, and your raw submissions are never exposed.

How do the proactive signals work?

Each time the benchmark recomputes, lumi re-reads your position and compares it to last time. A signal fires when something crosses a threshold — your percentile moves across a band, a practice becomes common among peers that you don't offer, or a gap to the market widens. It is ranked by market gap and emailed to the right people, and you can snooze or dismiss any of them. Every signal carries the evidence and sample size behind it.

Is our data safe?

Yes. Any figure resting on fewer than 5 organisations is suppressed everywhere — on screen, in exports and in board packs. Comparisons are medians and percentiles, so no single organisation's numbers can be reverse-engineered. Your data is used only for the shared benchmark, never sold, and handled under UK GDPR.

Do you benchmark salaries?

No — and that's deliberate. lumi benchmarks the rest of reward: benefits, time off, pension, wellbeing, recognition, and the policies and practices around pay — the things a salary survey never covers. It's how you tell the board whether your whole package is competitive, not just base pay.

How is this different from a reward survey?

A survey is a snapshot in one fixed peer cut, delivered months later as a PDF. lumi is a living benchmark: your position updates as members contribute, signals proactively email you when it changes, you can re-cut the peer group yourself in seconds, and every figure is cited — so it survives boardroom scrutiny.

Do we need to integrate anything?

No. lumi is a guided questionnaire — roughly an hour of answers, nothing to connect. Because we benchmark reward practices and policies (not individual employee records), there is no HRIS integration, no data pulled from your systems, and far less for IT and InfoSec to review.

Do we have to contribute our own data?

Yes — that's the co-operative deal, and it's what keeps the benchmark real. You only see comparisons for questions you've answered yourself, and "not applicable" is a valid answer. Most teams find the first meaningful comparison appears within minutes of starting.

How long does it take to get value?

Your first answer produces a live comparison immediately. A focused hour of data entry typically unlocks a meaningful position across the core domains — and the board pack can be generated the same day.

What does it cost?

£5,000 a year for full membership — unlimited users and usage, a fraction of one traditional reward survey. £10,000 if you'd rather not contribute your own data. Because members share the cost of the pool, it stays well below a survey or a consultant. Full pricing
